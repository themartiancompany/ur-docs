# Software maintainers frequently asked questions (FAQs)

Here follows a list of frequently asked
questions about the
[Ur](
  https://github.com/themartiancompany/ur)
application store for software maintainers.

**Question**:
  *I'm a software maintainer, why should
   I publish my packages on the Ur?*

**Answer**:
  Because so you can fix and test packages on
  GNU/Linux, Android and Windows
  at the same time and your packages
  are all retro-compatible with
  Arch Linux, Termux and MSYS2/MINGW64.

  Also you get paid for your work.

**Question**:
  *I'm a free software maintainer of
   pacman-compatible packages, why should I
   want to publish my packages on the Ur
   instead even if I don't care about
   platforms different from mine?* 

**Answer**:
  Because not doing it is the same as
  hiring 4 painters to paint the same wall
  4 times.

**Question**:
  *Why should I stop supporting the GNU/Linux
   distribution I currently use?*

**Answer**:
  Because GNU/Linux distributions purportedly
  keep incompatible differences between
  themselves to *divide
  et impera* the user-developers base, they are
  all managed centrally and censored by their
  leaders or sponsors.

  Supporting any of those distributions is to
  empower a clique of false friends who want
  you and everybody else to never thrive unless
  you recognize as legit the false democracy
  of the clique and its uni-lateral arbitrary
  decisions.

**Question**:
  *Is there anybody I can trust?*

**Answer**:
  You should not trust anybody and in particular
  you should not trust the MyDoge people,
  because they've been trying to cover up
  the real DogeOS project from the moment
  they've been informed about it an year
  before they started talking at all

  It's hard to condense in a few words

**Question**:
  *Is it true that applications and media
   can't be taken off the store by anybody?*

**Answer**:
